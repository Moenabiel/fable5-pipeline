# Ethics & Responsible Use

## Purpose
This project demonstrates that structured multi-step reasoning pipelines can be built entirely from open resources. It is intended for:
- Education and research
- Prototyping and experimentation
- Democratizing access to advanced AI reasoning patterns

## Responsible Use Guidelines

1. **Do not use for**: Generating misleading content, impersonating real people, creating spam, or any harmful purpose.
2. **Verify outputs**: All AI-generated content should be fact-checked before use in decision-making.
3. **Respect licenses**: Model weights have specific licenses — follow them.
4. **No proprietary redistribution**: Do not bundle or redistribute proprietary model weights.
5. **Transparency**: When using outputs from this pipeline, disclose that AI was involved.

## Limitations
- This pipeline approximates behavior, not capability — it is not equivalent to Claude Fable 5.
- Verifier checks are heuristic and may miss subtle errors.
- Hallucination detection is best-effort, not guaranteed.
- Free-tier models have context length and quality limits.

## Contact
For ethical concerns or misuse reports, open an issue on the repository.
